London Tourism

Buckingham Palace

Buckingham Palace is the official residence and administrative headquarters of the British monarch. The palace is used for official events and receptions, and is also open to the public for tours. Visitors can explore the State Rooms, which are used for official events and receptions, and see the famous Changing of the Guard ceremony.

The British Museum

The British Museum is one of the world's greatest museums, with a collection of over 8 million objects from all corners of the globe. The museum's collection includes everything from ancient Egyptian mummies to Greek and Roman artifacts, and is a must-visit for anyone interested in history and culture.

The London Eye

The London Eye is a giant Ferris wheel located on the South Bank of the River Thames. The wheel is 135 meters tall and offers stunning views of the city from its 32 glass-enclosed capsules. Visitors can enjoy a 30-minute ride and take in the sights of London from a unique perspective.

The National Gallery

The National Gallery is a world-class art museum that houses a collection of over 2,300 paintings, including works by artists such as Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Vincent van Gogh. The museum is located in Trafalgar Square and is free to visit, making it a must-see for anyone interested in art and culture.

The Tower of London

The Tower of London is a historic fortress located on the north bank of the River Thames. The tower has served as a palace, prison, armory, and even a zoo over the centuries, and is now home to the Crown Jewels. Visitors can explore the tower and see the famous Yeoman Warders (also known as Beefeaters) and the iconic ravens that live on the premises.
