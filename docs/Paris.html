Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower: The iconic symbol of Paris, the Eiffel Tower is a must-visit attraction. Standing at 324 meters tall, it offers breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum: One of the world's largest and most famous museums, the Louvre is home to an impressive collection of art and artifacts, including the Mona Lisa.

Notre-Dame Cathedral: A beautiful and historic cathedral, Notre-Dame is a masterpiece of Gothic architecture and a popular tourist destination.

The Arc de Triomphe: A monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France, the Arc de Triomphe is a symbol of French history and culture.

The Seine River: A scenic river that runs through the heart of Paris, the Seine River offers stunning views of the city and is a popular spot for boat cruises and walking tours.
