Paris Tourism

The Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris, standing at 324 meters tall. It was built for the 1889 World's Fair and has since become a symbol of the city. Visitors can take a lift to the top for breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing a vast collection of art and artifacts from around the world. The museum is home to the Mona Lisa, as well as many other famous works of art.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. It is known for its stunning architecture and beautiful stained glass windows. Visitors can take a tour of the cathedral and climb to the top for views of the city.

Montmartre

Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood in Paris, known for its narrow streets, charming cafes, and stunning views of the city. Visitors can explore the neighborhood, visit the Sacre-Coeur Basilica, and enjoy the street performers and artists.

Champs-Élysées

Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous streets in Paris, known for its upscale shops, cafes, and theaters. Visitors can stroll along the street, visit the Arc de Triomphe, and enjoy the street performers and artists.
