Top Attractions in Paris

  • The Eiffel Tower

    The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris and a must-visit attraction. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, the tower stands at an impressive 324 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the city.

  • The Louvre Museum

    The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning glass pyramid entrance is a work of art in itself.

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral

    Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. The cathedral's stunning Gothic architecture and intricate stone carvings make it a must-see attraction in Paris.

  • Montmartre

    Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood in Paris that is famous for its bohemian vibe, street artists, and stunning views of the city. Visitors can explore the narrow streets, visit the Sacre Coeur Basilica, and enjoy the area's many cafes and restaurants.

  • Champs-Élysées

    Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous streets in the world, lined with upscale boutiques, cafes, and theaters. Visitors can stroll along the street, take in the sights and sounds, and enjoy the area's many restaurants and bars.
