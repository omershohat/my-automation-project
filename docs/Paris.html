Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris, standing at 324 meters tall. It was built for the 1889 World's Fair and has become a symbol of the city.

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing a vast collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa.

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that was built in the 12th century. It is one of the most famous and visited landmarks in Paris, known for its stunning architecture and beautiful stained glass windows.

The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. It is located in the center of the city and offers stunning views of the surrounding area.
