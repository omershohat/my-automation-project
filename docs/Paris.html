Recommended Attractions in Paris

1. Louvre Museum

Explore the world's largest art museum and one of the most visited museums in the world.

2. Notre-Dame Cathedral

Visit the historic and iconic Notre-Dame Cathedral, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

3. Eiffel Tower

See the famous Eiffel Tower, a global icon and symbol of Paris.

4. Montmartre

Explore the charming Montmartre neighborhood, home to the famous Moulin Rouge and Sacré-Cœur Basilica.

5. Champs-Élysées

Wander along the famous Champs-Élysées avenue, lined with luxury shops and the Arc de Triomphe.
