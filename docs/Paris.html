Top Attractions in Paris

  • The Eiffel Tower

    The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris and a must-visit attraction. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, the tower stands at 324 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the city.

  • The Louvre Museum

    The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing a vast collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning architecture and beautiful gardens make it a must-visit destination.

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral

    Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. The cathedral's stunning architecture and beautiful stained glass windows make it a must-visit attraction in Paris.

  • Montmartre

    Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood in Paris, known for its narrow streets, charming cafes, and stunning views of the city. The neighborhood is also home to the beautiful Sacre-Coeur Basilica.

  • Champs-Élysées

    Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous streets in Paris, known for its upscale shopping, dining, and entertainment options. The street is lined with beautiful cafes, restaurants, and theaters, making it a must-visit destination for anyone visiting Paris.
