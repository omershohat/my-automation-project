Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris, standing at 324 meters tall. Visitors can take the elevator to the top for breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum is home to some of the world's most famous artworks, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning glass pyramid entrance is a must-see.

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. Visitors can take a guided tour of the cathedral and climb to the top for stunning views of the city.

The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. Visitors can climb to the top for panoramic views of the city.

The Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous shopping streets in the world, lined with high-end boutiques, cafes, and restaurants. Visitors can stroll along the street and take in the sights and sounds of the city.
