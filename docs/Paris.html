Things to Do in Paris

Explore the city's rich history and culture by visiting iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral.

Eiffel Tower

Admire the stunning view from the top of the Eiffel Tower, the world's most visited paid monument.

Louvre Museum

Visit the Louvre Museum to see the famous Mona Lisa painting and thousands of other works of art.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Wander through the stunning Notre-Dame Cathedral, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
