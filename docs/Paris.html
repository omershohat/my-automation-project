Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris and a must-visit attraction. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it stands at an impressive 324 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning glass pyramid entrance is a work of art in itself.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. Its stunning Gothic architecture and stunning rose windows make it a must-visit attraction in Paris.

The Arc de Triomphe

The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. It offers stunning views of the city and is a great spot to watch the sunset.

The Seine River

The Seine River runs through the heart of Paris and offers a unique perspective on the city's landmarks and bridges. Take a river cruise or stroll along the riverbank to experience the city's beauty and charm.
