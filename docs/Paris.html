Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris, standing at 324 meters tall. Visitors can take a lift to the top for breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum is home to some of the world's most famous artworks, including the Mona Lisa. With a collection of over 550,000 works of art, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. Although it suffered a devastating fire in 2019, it remains one of the most popular tourist attractions in Paris.

The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. Visitors can climb to the top for stunning views of the city.

The Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous streets in the world, lined with cafes, restaurants, and shops. It's the perfect place to relax and people-watch.
