Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris and a must-visit attraction. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, the tower stands at an impressive 324 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning architecture and beautiful gardens make it a must-visit destination.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. The cathedral's stunning architecture and beautiful stained glass windows make it a must-visit destination for anyone interested in history, architecture, or religion.

Montmartre

Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood in Paris that is known for its bohemian vibe, street artists, and stunning views of the city. Visitors can explore the narrow streets, visit the beautiful Sacre-Coeur Basilica, and enjoy the lively atmosphere.

Champs-Élysées

Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous streets in the world, known for its upscale shopping, dining, and entertainment options. Visitors can stroll along the street, visit the Arc de Triomphe, and enjoy the lively atmosphere.
