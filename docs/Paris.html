Top Attractions in Paris

  • The Eiffel Tower

    The Eiffel Tower is an iconic symbol of Paris and one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it stands at 324 meters tall and offers breathtaking views of the city.

  • The Louvre Museum

    The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing a vast collection of art and artifacts from ancient civilizations to the 19th century. The museum's stunning glass pyramid entrance is a must-see.

  • Notre-Dame Cathedral

    Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. Its stunning Gothic architecture and stunning rose windows make it a must-visit attraction in Paris.

  • Arc de Triomphe

    The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. It offers stunning views of the city from its rooftop and is a great spot to watch the sunset.

  • Montmartre

    Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood that is famous for its bohemian vibe, street artists, and stunning views of the city. It's a great place to explore and get a feel for the real Paris.
