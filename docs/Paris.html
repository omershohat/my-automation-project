Top Attractions in Paris

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris, standing at 324 meters tall. Visitors can take a lift to the top for breathtaking views of the city.

The Louvre Museum is home to an impressive collection of art and artifacts, including the Mona Lisa. The museum's stunning glass pyramid entrance is a must-see.

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. Its stunning architecture and tranquil atmosphere make it a must-visit attraction in Paris.

The Arc de Triomphe is a monumental arch that honors the soldiers who fought and died for France. Visitors can climb to the top for stunning views of the city.

The Seine River is a picturesque waterway that runs through the heart of Paris. Visitors can take a river cruise or stroll along the riverbank to take in the city's sights and sounds.
