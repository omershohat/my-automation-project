Paris Attractions

Top Attractions in Paris

Eiffel Tower

The iconic Eiffel Tower is a must-see attraction in Paris. Admire the stunning view from the top of the tower or take a stroll around its base. The tower was built for the 1889 World's Fair and remains a symbol of Paris to this day.

Mont Sainte-Chapelle

Visit the stunning Mont Sainte-Chapelle, a medieval Gothic cathedral that showcases the finest examples of Gothic architecture. Be amazed by the intricate details and stunning views.

Notre-Dame de Paris

Explore the historic Notre-Dame de Paris, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a masterpiece of Gothic architecture. Don't miss the stunning views from the towers.

Champs-Élysées

Walk along the picturesque Champs-Élysées avenue, lined with elegant shops, cafes, and historical landmarks. Enjoy the bustling atmosphere and beautiful architecture.

Seine River

Take a stroll along the Seine River, which winds through the heart of Paris, and admire the city's skyline. Don't forget to snap a photo with the Eiffel Tower in the background.

Arc de Triomphe

Visit the iconic Arc de Triomphe, a triumphal arch built in honor of French victories. Enjoy the stunning view of the city from the top of the arch.

Montmartre

Explore the charming neighborhood of Montmartre, known for its bohemian atmosphere and stunning views of the city. Visit the Sacré-Cœur Basilica for a panoramic view.

Eiffel Tower

Climb to the top of the Eiffel Tower for a breathtaking view of the city and the Seine river.

Louvre Museum

Visit the Louvre Museum to admire the world's finest art collection.
