Paris Tourism

The Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Paris and a must-visit attraction. Built for the 1889 World's Fair, it stands at an impressive 324 meters tall and offers stunning views of the city.

The Louvre Museum

The Louvre Museum is one of the world's largest and most famous museums, housing an impressive collection of art and artifacts from around the world, including the Mona Lisa.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Notre-Dame Cathedral is a beautiful and historic church that dates back to the 12th century. It's known for its stunning architecture and stunning stained glass windows.

Montmartre

Montmartre is a charming and historic neighborhood in Paris, known for its narrow streets, charming cafes, and stunning views of the city. It's a great place to explore and get a feel for the local culture.

Champs-Élysées

Champs-Élysées is one of the most famous shopping streets in the world, lined with high-end boutiques, cafes, and theaters. It's a great place to shop, dine, and people-watch.
