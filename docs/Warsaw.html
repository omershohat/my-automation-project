Warsaw Tourism

Old Town

Warsaw's Old Town is a historic district that was rebuilt after World War II. It features colorful buildings, narrow streets, and a charming atmosphere. Visitors can explore the Royal Castle, St. John's Archcathedral, and the Old Town Square.

Warsaw Uprising Museum

The Warsaw Uprising Museum is a must-visit attraction for anyone interested in history. It tells the story of the Warsaw Uprising, a heroic but ultimately doomed rebellion against the Nazi occupation during World War II. The museum features interactive exhibits, personal stories, and a collection of artifacts.

Lazienki Park

Lazienki Park is a beautiful green space in the heart of Warsaw. It features stunning gardens, lakes, and walking trails, as well as several historic buildings, including the stunning Lazienki Palace. Visitors can rent a boat, attend a concert, or simply relax in the park's peaceful atmosphere.

Praga District

The Praga District is a charming and eclectic neighborhood that is home to a mix of historic buildings, street art, and trendy bars and restaurants. Visitors can explore the district's narrow streets, visit the Praga Museum, and enjoy the area's lively nightlife.

National Museum

The National Museum in Warsaw is one of the largest and most important museums in Poland. It features a vast collection of art and artifacts, including works by Polish and international artists, as well as exhibits on the country's history and culture.
