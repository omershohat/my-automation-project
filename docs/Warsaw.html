Warsaw Tourism

Top Attractions in Warsaw

Old Town

Warsaw's Old Town is a historic neighborhood that was rebuilt after World War II. It features colorful buildings, narrow streets, and a lively atmosphere. Visitors can walk along the Royal Route, visit the Old Town Square, and explore the nearby castle.

Warsaw Uprising Museum

The Warsaw Uprising Museum is a museum that commemorates the Warsaw Uprising, a heroic but ultimately doomed rebellion against the Nazi occupation during World War II. The museum features interactive exhibits, films, and personal stories that bring the history to life.

Lazienki Park

Lazienki Park is a beautiful royal park that features stunning palaces, gardens, and lakes. Visitors can walk through the park, visit the Palace on the Water, and enjoy the scenery. The park is also home to the famous Chopin monument, where free piano concerts are held during the summer.

Praga District

The Praga District is a trendy neighborhood that is known for its street art, independent shops, and vibrant nightlife. Visitors can explore the district's many bars, clubs, and restaurants, and enjoy the lively atmosphere.

National Museum

The National Museum is a museum that features a collection of Polish art and artifacts from the Middle Ages to the present day. The museum's collection includes works by famous Polish artists, as well as exhibits on Polish history and culture.
