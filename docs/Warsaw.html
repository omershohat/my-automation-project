Warsaw Tourism

Old Town

The historic heart of Warsaw, Old Town is a must-visit attraction. The area is filled with colorful buildings, charming streets, and historic landmarks like the Royal Castle and St. John's Archcathedral.

Warsaw Uprising Museum

This museum tells the story of the Warsaw Uprising, a heroic but ultimately doomed rebellion against the Nazi occupation during World War II. The museum features interactive exhibits, personal stories, and a collection of artifacts.

Łazienki Park

This beautiful park is a peaceful oasis in the heart of the city. It features stunning gardens, a lake, and several historic buildings, including the stunning Palace on the Water.

Praga District

This up-and-coming neighborhood is known for its street art, indie cafes, and vibrant nightlife. It's a great place to explore and experience the city's alternative culture.

National Museum

This world-class museum features a vast collection of Polish art and artifacts, including works by famous artists like Jan Matejko and Stanisław Witkiewicz. The museum also hosts temporary exhibitions and events.
