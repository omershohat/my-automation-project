Jerusalem Tourism

Western Wall

The Western Wall, also known as the Wailing Wall, is a sacred site in Jerusalem and one of the most significant landmarks in the Jewish faith. It is a remnant of the ancient Second Temple complex and a place of great spiritual importance.

Dome of the Rock

The Dome of the Rock is a stunning example of Islamic architecture and one of the most recognizable landmarks in Jerusalem. It is a shrine built on the site where the Prophet Muhammad is believed to have ascended to heaven.

Church of the Holy Sepulchre

The Church of the Holy Sepulchre is a significant site in Christianity and is believed to be the location of Jesus' crucifixion, burial, and resurrection. It is a complex of chapels and shrines that reflect the diverse traditions and denominations of the Christian faith.

Jerusalem Old City

The Jerusalem Old City is a historic and culturally rich area that is home to many significant landmarks, including the Western Wall, the Dome of the Rock, and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a must-visit destination for anyone interested in history, culture, and spirituality.

Mount of Olives

The Mount of Olives is a significant site in Christianity and offers breathtaking views of the Jerusalem skyline. It is a place of great spiritual importance and is believed to be the location of Jesus' ascension to heaven.
